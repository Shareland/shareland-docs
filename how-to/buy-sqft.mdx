<Note>
  You'll need USDC on the **Base** network to trade. There is no in-app on-ramp. Acquire USDC on Base through your wallet provider before getting started.
</Note>

## Step 1: Browse markets

From the exchange, browse the available markets. Each card shows the current oracle price (\$/sqft), 24h volume, and recent price movement.

## Step 2: Select a market

Click any market card to open the detail panel on the right. You'll see:
- Live price chart and oracle price
- Neighborhood breakdown with individual area prices
- Community posts from other traders
- Your current position in this market (if any)

<Frame caption="Shareland: Market detail panel (New York)">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/market-detail.png" alt="Market detail panel" />
</Frame>

## Step 3: Open the action panel

Click **Trade** in the market action panel to open the buy/sell interface.

## Step 4: Choose order type

The trade panel has two tabs: **Market** and **Limit**.

### Market order (default)

Select the **Buy** tab under **Market**. Enter the USDC amount you want to trade. The interface shows you:
- Number of SQFT tokens you'll receive
- 1.5% transaction fee
- Total cost

Market orders execute immediately at the current AMM price.

### Limit order

Select the **Limit** tab to set a specific price. Enter:
- **Price** — the maximum $/sqft you're willing to pay
- **Amount** — USDC to spend or SQFT tokens to buy

Your order sits in the order book until the market reaches your price. You can view and cancel open orders from the **Orders** tab.

There is no minimum trade size. A minimum fee of \$0.10 applies per transaction.

<Frame caption="Shareland: Buy trade panel">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/trade.png" alt="Trade panel: buy" />
</Frame>

## Step 5: Confirm

Review the details and confirm. Market orders settle on-chain in seconds. Limit orders remain open until filled or cancelled. Your position appears in your portfolio immediately after execution.

<Check>
  Your new position is visible instantly in the Portfolio tab.
</Check>
